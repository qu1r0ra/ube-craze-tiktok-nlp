# Privacy and artifact notes

The course collected public social-media material. Public accessibility alone does not settle personal-data processing, reuse, or redistribution obligations. The Philippine National Privacy Commission's [2026 web-scraping guidance](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SGD_A_1.pdf) is relevant to the assessment; this repository does not claim that maintenance establishes compliance or supplies a lawful basis.

The public-tree correction removes comment/creator registries, verbatim sample banks, copied media, derivative editor packages, and unreviewed text-bearing images. Original reports and course deliverables are not replaced with invented findings. The synthetic demonstration is labelled separately and reads no source corpus.

Historical commits remain a separate exposure. The proposed next step is a recoverable rewrite of affected refs after the ordinary correction is accepted and the exact paths/refs are reviewed. Until then, historical removal is pending and public profile linking is withheld. A rewrite cannot recall forks, clones, external Drive copies, caches, or already downloaded media. The local pre-remediation bundle preserves recovery evidence and must remain private.

Local analysis and media utilities can still produce sensitive artifacts under ignored `data/`. Review notebooks before saving outputs and review every proposed public artifact. Ignore rules reduce accidental staging; they are not a privacy guarantee. New data collection, external document changes, repository deletion, visibility changes, and force-pushes are outside this tree correction.
